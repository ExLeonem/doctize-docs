Choosing an eviction policy

A bounded cache must decide what to throw away when it is full. Acme Cache offers three policies, selected with dev.example.cache.CacheBuilder.evictionPolicy.

Policy Evicts Best for
EvictionPolicy.LRU the entry not used for the longest time most workloads
EvictionPolicy.LFU the entry used least often stable hot sets with bursts of one-off reads
EvictionPolicy.FIFO the oldest entry data with a natural time-to-live

How to decide#

Start with LRU. Record statistics and look at the hit rate:

var cache = Cache.<String, Page>builder().maximumSize(1_000).recordStats().build();
// ... run your workload ...
System.out.printf("hit rate: %.1f%%%n", cache.stats().hitRate() * 100);

If a full table scan pushes the hot entries out, try LFU.

Expiry is independent#

Expiry (time to live) is checked on every read and works with every policy.
